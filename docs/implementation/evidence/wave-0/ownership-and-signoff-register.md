## Appointment acceptance

Each named appointment must record:

- full name and role/title;
- legal company or group authority;
- email/phone through an approved internal directory, not this public repository;
- decision and approval scope;
- substitute/delegate and expiry where applicable;
- acceptance date;
- conflicts or segregation-of-duties restrictions.

Personal contact information and signatures must be retained in the governed project record, not committed to source control.
