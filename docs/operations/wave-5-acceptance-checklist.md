This checklist is an approval record, not documentation that can be pre-checked
by engineering. Attach immutable evidence references and identity-provider
subjects for every signer.

- [ ] Migration lead approves source register, classification, mapping version and cutoff procedure.
- [ ] Data owners approve cleansed organizations, users, parties, products, units, prices, taxes, accounts, employees and open documents.
- [ ] Finance and Inventory approve all ten reconciliations for Production Trial 1.
- [ ] Finance and Inventory approve all ten reconciliations for timed Production Trial 2.
- [ ] Payroll owner and Tanzanian payroll professional approve parallel-run and statutory/accounting reconciliation.
- [ ] Product and Operations approve maker-checker UAT completed without implementation-team substitution.
- [ ] Accessibility and language owners approve English, Swahili, printing and device-matrix results.
- [ ] Platform owner approves load, soak, restore and disaster-recovery evidence against approved objectives.
- [ ] Security owner approves independent penetration test and dependency results with no critical/high finding.
- [ ] QA owner confirms no critical/high defect and records owner, workaround and approval for every accepted lower defect.
- [ ] Finance, Tax, Payroll, Operations, Migration, Security and Executive owners approve the pilot candidate.

Until every item is checked with retained evidence,
`release_exit_status=BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`.
